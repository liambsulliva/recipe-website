### Ingredients

- 4 medium-sized flour tortillas (8-inch)
- 1/2 cup marinara sauce
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/4 cup turkey pepperoni slices
- 1 cup mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

### Steps

1. Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a small skillet, sauté the mushrooms with a little olive oil until they release their moisture and begin to brown, about 3-4 minutes. Set aside.
3. Lightly brush one side of each tortilla with olive oil.
4. Place a tortilla, oil side down, in the air fryer basket.
5. Spread 2 tablespoons of pizza sauce evenly over the tortilla, leaving a small border around the edge.
6. Sprinkle about 1/3 cup of mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
7. Top with turkey pepperoni slices and sautéed mushrooms.
8. Air fry for 5-6 minutes until the cheese is melted and the edges of the tortilla are golden and crispy.
9. Carefully remove from the air fryer using a spatula.
10. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
11. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.
12. Allow to cool slightly before slicing, or the pizza will fall apart.

**Note:** To ensure your ingredients stick, add an extra layer of cheese on your toppings so they don't fly off while cooking.
