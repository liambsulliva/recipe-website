### Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 2 tbsp milk or water
- 1/4 cup smoked trout, flaked
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 oz cream cheese, small cubes (optional)
- 1 tsp fresh dill, chopped (or 1/4 tsp dried)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Lemon wedges for serving

### Steps

1. In a bowl, whisk together eggs and milk until well combined. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat until foaming subsides.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan, tilting to ensure even coverage.
4. As the eggs begin to set (about 1-2 minutes), sprinkle the flaked smoked trout, green onions, cream cheese cubes, and dill evenly over one half of the omelette.
5. When the eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny on top, use a spatula to fold the empty half over the filling.
6. Cook for another 30 seconds, then slide onto a plate.
7. Garnish with additional green onions and serve with lemon wedges for a bright finish.

### Notes

- For a richer flavor, add a small dollop of sour cream or crème fraîche to the filling.
- Smoked salmon can be substituted for the trout if preferred.
- This pairs wonderfully with a light salad or toasted sourdough bread.
