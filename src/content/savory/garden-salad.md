### Ingredients

* 4 cups mixed greens (spinach, arugula, kale)
* 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
* 1 cup chickpeas, rinsed and drained
* 1 can chicken breast
* 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
* 1/4 cup walnuts or almonds, chopped
* 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
* 1/2 cucumber, diced
* 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
* 1 avocado, sliced

### Dressing

* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tbsp lemon juice
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* Salt and pepper to taste
* 1 tsp honey (optional)

### Steps

1. In a large bowl, combine all salad ingredients except the chicken.
2. In a small jar, combine all dressing ingredients and shake well until emulsified.
3. Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently to coat.
4. Add sliced avocado on top just before serving.
5. Serve immediately.

### Notes

* For a vegetarian option, replace the chicken with 1/2 cup of quinoa or additional chickpeas.
* Add edamame or hemp seeds for an extra protein boost.
* This salad keeps well in the refrigerator for up to 2 days if the dressing is stored separately.
