### Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup milk of choice
- 2 tbsp natural peanut butter
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

**For the Raspberry Chia Jam:**

- 1 cup fresh raspberries
- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- 2 tbsp maple syrup

### Steps

1. In a bowl, mash the raspberries with a fork until mostly broken down.
2. Add chia seeds, maple syrup, and lemon juice to the raspberries. Mix well and set aside for 10 minutes to thicken.
3. In a separate bowl, combine oats, Greek yogurt, milk, peanut butter, honey/maple syrup, vanilla extract, and salt. Stir until well mixed.
4. In two mason jars or containers, layer the oat mixture and raspberry chia jam, starting and ending with the oat mixture.
5. Seal the containers and refrigerate overnight or for at least 4 hours.
6. Before serving, top with additional fresh raspberries, a drizzle of peanut butter, and a sprinkle of crushed peanuts if desired.
