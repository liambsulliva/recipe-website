### Ingredients

#### For the bowl:

- 2 cans (12.5 oz each) chicken breast, drained
- 4 cups spring mix
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 avocado, diced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup corn kernels (canned or frozen, thawed)
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

#### For the Greek yogurt lemon dill sauce:

- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Steps

1. In a small bowl, prepare the sauce by combining Greek yogurt, chopped dill, lemon juice, minced garlic, and honey. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir well and refrigerate until ready to use.

2. Heat the canned chicken in a microwave-safe bowl for about 1 minute or until warm. Season with salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lime juice.

3. If desired, warm the black beans in a small saucepan over medium heat with a pinch of cumin and garlic powder.

4. To assemble the bowls, divide the spring mix between 4 serving bowls.

5. Top each bowl with equal portions of chicken, black beans, cherry tomatoes, diced avocado, red onion slices, and corn.

6. Drizzle the Greek yogurt lemon dill sauce over each bowl.

7. Garnish with chopped cilantro and serve with lime wedges on the side.

**Variations:**

- Add cooked rice or quinoa for a more filling meal
- Include crushed tortilla chips for extra texture
- Substitute the dill in the sauce with chipotle powder for a smoky flavor
- Add sliced cucumber to the dill sauce to give it more body
