### Ingredients

- 2 ripe peaches, halved and pitted
- 1 tbsp melted butter or coconut oil
- 2 tsp brown sugar or coconut sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon, plus more for dusting
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey, plus more for drizzling
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup mixed nuts (walnuts, pecans, almonds), roughly chopped
- Fresh mint leaves for garnish (optional)

### Steps

1. Preheat your air fryer to 375°F (190°C) for about 3 minutes.
2. In a small bowl, mix together the melted butter/oil, brown sugar, and cinnamon.
3. Brush the cut sides of the peach halves with the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
4. Place the peach halves cut side up in the air fryer basket, making sure they don't overlap.
5. Air fry for 7-9 minutes until the peaches are tender and caramelized on top.
6. While the peaches are cooking, mix the Greek yogurt with vanilla extract and 1 tbsp of honey in a small bowl.
7. Toast the chopped nuts in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3-5 minutes until fragrant, stirring frequently to prevent burning.
8. To serve, place the warm peach halves on plates, top each with a generous dollop of the honey-vanilla Greek yogurt.
9. Sprinkle with the toasted nuts, dust with a little extra cinnamon, and drizzle with additional honey.
10. Garnish with fresh mint leaves if desired and serve immediately while still warm.

**Note:** For a more decadent dessert, you can substitute the Greek yogurt with vanilla ice cream.
