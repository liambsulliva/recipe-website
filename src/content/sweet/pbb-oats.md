### Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup milk of choice
- 2 tbsp natural peanut butter
- 1 ripe banana, mashed (plus slices for topping)
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

**Optional Toppings:**

- Sliced banana
- Drizzle of peanut butter
- Chopped walnuts or almonds
- Hemp seeds or chia seeds

### Steps

1. In a medium bowl, mash the ripe banana with a fork until smooth.
2. Add Greek yogurt, milk, peanut butter, honey/maple syrup, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and salt. Stir until well combined.
3. Fold in the rolled oats and mix thoroughly.
4. Divide the mixture evenly between two mason jars or containers.
5. Seal the containers and refrigerate overnight or for at least 4 hours.
6. Before serving, top with sliced banana, a drizzle of peanut butter, and optional nuts or seeds.
7. Enjoy cold or warm it up slightly in the microwave if preferred.
