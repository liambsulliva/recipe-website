### Ingredients

- 4 Salmon Fillets (6oz each)
- 3 tbsp Olive Oil
- 2 tbsp Dijon Mustard
- 2 tbsp Honey
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp Fresh Dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
- 1/2 tsp Salt
- 1/4 tsp Black Pepper
- 1 Lemon, sliced

### Steps

1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400°F).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, mustard, honey, garlic, dill, parsley, salt, and pepper.
3. Brush the marinade generously over both sides of salmon fillets.
4. Place salmon skin-side down on preheated grill grates.
5. Grill for 4-5 minutes, then carefully flip and grill for another 3-4 minutes.
6. Remove when internal temperature reaches 145°F.
7. Serve with lemon slices and extra herbs.
