### Ingredients

- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup honey
- ¼ cup dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon salt

### Steps

1. In a medium bowl, combine the Greek yogurt, honey, dijon mustard, and lemon juice.
2. Add the paprika, onion powder, and salt.
3. Whisk all ingredients together until smooth and well combined.
4. Taste and adjust seasonings if needed.
5. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld.
6. Store in the refrigerator for up to one week.

**Serving suggestions:** Use as a salad dressing, a dip for vegetables or chicken tenders, or as a spread on sandwiches.
