### Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs Flank Steak, sliced thin
- 3 tbsp Soy Sauce
- 2 tbsp Oyster Sauce
- 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
- 2 tbsp Cornstarch
- 4 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp Ginger, grated
- 2 Bell Peppers, sliced
- 1 Onion, sliced
- 2 cups Broccoli Florets
- 2 tbsp Vegetable Oil
- 1/4 cup Green Onions, chopped

### Steps

1. Slice flank steak against the grain into thin strips.
2. Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and cornstarch in a bowl.
3. Marinate beef in half the sauce mixture for 15 minutes.
4. Heat vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
5. Stir-fry beef in batches until browned, about 2-3 minutes per batch.
6. Remove beef and add garlic and ginger to the pan.
7. Add vegetables and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
8. Return beef to pan and add remaining sauce mixture.
9. Cook for 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens.
10. Garnish with green onions and serve hot.
