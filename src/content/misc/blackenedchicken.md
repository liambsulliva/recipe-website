### Ingredients

- 4 Chicken Breasts (6-8oz each)
- 2 tbsp Paprika
- 1 tbsp Cayenne Pepper
- 1 tbsp Garlic Powder
- 1 tbsp Onion Powder
- 1 tbsp Dried Thyme
- 1 tbsp Dried Oregano
- 1 tsp Salt
- 1 tsp Black Pepper
- 3 tbsp Butter
- 2 tbsp Olive Oil

### Steps

1. Mix all dry spices in a small bowl to create blackening seasoning.
2. Pat chicken breasts dry with paper towels.
3. Generously coat both sides of chicken with the spice mixture.
4. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking.
5. Add butter and olive oil to the hot skillet.
6. Carefully place chicken breasts in the skillet.
7. Cook for 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
8. Let rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
