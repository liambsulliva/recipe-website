### Ingredients

- 3 large eggs, whites and yolks separated
- 1 scoop (about 30g) vanilla or unflavored protein powder
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp cream of tartar (helps stabilize egg whites)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

**Optional Toppings:**
- Fresh berries
- Sliced banana
- Greek yogurt
- Chopped nuts

### Steps

1. Separate the egg whites from the yolks, placing them in different bowls.
2. Add cream of tartar to the egg whites and beat with an electric mixer until soft peaks form.
3. In the bowl with egg yolks, whisk in the protein powder, coconut flour (if using), baking powder, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt until smooth.
4. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the yolk mixture using a spatula. Work in batches, folding carefully to maintain the air in the egg whites.
5. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat and add coconut oil or butter.
6. Pour the batter into the pan to form a large pancake (or smaller ones if preferred).
7. Cook for 2-3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface and the bottom is golden brown.
8. Carefully flip and cook for another 2 minutes until cooked through.
9. Transfer to a plate, drizzle with maple syrup and add your choice of toppings.

### Notes

- For chocolate protein pancakes, use chocolate protein powder
- The key to fluffy pancakes is to fold the egg whites gently to maintain their volume.
- Cook on medium-low heat to ensure the pancake cooks through without burning.
- These pancakes are more delicate than traditional ones, so flip carefully.
